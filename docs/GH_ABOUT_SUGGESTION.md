Research-stage nanopositioning platform with prototype digital-twin models and UQ workflows. Includes modeling for Casimir-aware control; not production-ready.

Long description:
This repository contains research and prototype implementations for high-precision nanopositioning, including digital-twin models, UQ analyses, and control experiments. Reported performance figures are from controlled experiments or simulations and are provided for reproducibility and research use. Maintain clear provenance when citing results and attach raw data and analysis scripts in `docs/` when possible.
